**Download:** `Audiobook-Maker-…-mac-arm64.dmg` below. For Macs with Apple silicon (M1 or newer), macOS 12 or later.

1. Open the .dmg and drag **Audiobook Maker** into **Applications**.
2. The first time, macOS blocks it because it isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account. Open **System
   Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to Security and click **Open Anyway** next to Audiobook Maker. After
   that it opens normally.
3. Voices download the first time you use them (about 330 MB). **Settings → Download
   all voices** fetches them all at once.

This is a beta. If something goes wrong, open Settings, click **Copy details** and paste them into an issue.

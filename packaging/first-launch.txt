Audiobook Maker is free and isn't signed with a paid Apple developer account, so the first time you open it
macOS says it can't check it for malicious software. To open it:

1. Drag Audiobook Maker into the Applications folder, then double-click it. macOS says it can't be opened.
   Click Done (or OK).
2. Open System Settings, then Privacy & Security.
3. Scroll down to Security. Next to "Audiobook Maker was blocked", click Open Anyway, and confirm.

After that it opens normally, like any other app.

The source code of this exact version is on GitHub, under the version's tag.
